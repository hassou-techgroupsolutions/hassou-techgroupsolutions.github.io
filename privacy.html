BUSINESS WEBSITE / PRIVACY

Privacy notice.

This notice describes the inquiry and local-device features of this portfolio website. It is a website-level notice, not a substitute for a full organization-wide privacy policy.

What information is requested?

The contact form asks for your name, email address, type of project and project description. Please avoid sending passwords, identity documents, private patient/student data or confidential client records through this public form.

How are inquiries delivered?

When you submit the contact form, your information is sent to a third-party form-delivery provider (Formspree) for forwarding to the team. The provider has its own privacy terms. The team uses your information to review and respond to the inquiry.

What stays in your browser?

The interactive project planner processes your selections inside your browser only. It does not save its contents or transmit them until you choose to include the result in an inquiry. The theme setting may be stored locally to remember your display preference.

Retention, access and requests

For questions about submitted information, requests to correct or delete an inquiry, or the team’s information-handling practices, contact tgroupsolutions.dev2026@gmail.com. Specific retention periods and arrangements should be confirmed by the business administrator.

Third-party links and project screenshots

External services and project websites have their own privacy practices. Published project images are intended to illustrate software interfaces; please contact the team if a displayed image should be reviewed.
