07 / BEFORE YOU START
Questions worth asking early.
We prefer to clarify cost, scope, ownership and expectations before work begins.
Have another question? Contact us ↗
Is the initial assessment really free? +
Yes. The initial discussion and preliminary project review are free. Design, coding, testing, deployment or other implementation work is priced separately under an approved quotation.


Do you only create websites? +
No. The team has web, database-backed and application-based system experience. The supported platform and implementation approach are determined through discovery.


Can you fix an existing or unfinished system? +
Yes, subject to access, technical review and agreed scope. We can assess bugs, unfinished features, UI/UX, data workflows and supported deployment issues.


Can you help with a thesis or capstone project? +
We support implementation, enhancements, QA, documentation and demonstration preparation while students remain responsible for understanding and defending their own academic work.


How do pricing and revisions work? +
We provide a separate scope-based quotation. Milestones, inclusions, revision limits and potential scope changes are specified in the proposal or agreement.


Will we own the source code? +
Source-code rights, handover deliverables, hosting access, credentials and documentation are agreed in writing before work begins.


Can you maintain the system after launch? +
Deployment, handover and ongoing support can be included in the original scope or arranged as a separate service.
